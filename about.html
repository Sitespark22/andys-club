Our Story

Born from Tradition. Built for Ogden.

Andy's Club opened its doors in 1974 with a simple mission: serve honest, made-from-scratch Greek food the way it's meant to be made. No shortcuts, no compromises — just recipes rooted in Greek heritage and prepared fresh every day.

For over 50 years, generations of Ogden families have made Andy's their go-to for souvlaki, gyros, and authentic Mediterranean flavors. We're proud to be a cornerstone of this community, and even prouder that our recipes haven't changed a bit.

We're still family-owned and operated. When you visit Andy's Club, you're not eating at a chain — you're a guest in our family's kitchen.
